My Shooting Journey

Precision, focus and discipline — my journey in the sport of shooting spans from my early school years to competitive events during college.

School

Where It Began

Started shooting as part of my school's air rifle team, learning the fundamentals of stance, breathing control and aiming.

Inter-School Championships

Represented my school in inter-school shooting championships, securing podium finishes in the 10m air rifle category.

Discipline & Focus

Built the discipline and mental focus that shooting demands — skills that carried over into academics and everyday life.

College

College Team

Continued the sport at NIT Raipur, competing in inter-college tournaments and refining precision shooting techniques.

Tournaments & Fests

Participated in college sports fests and shooting meets, competing against skilled marksmen from across the region.

Team Spirit

Learned the value of teamwork and consistency while balancing shooting practice with a demanding IT curriculum.
